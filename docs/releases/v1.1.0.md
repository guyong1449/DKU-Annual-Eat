# v1.1.0

这一版修复了英文校园卡页面的采集与汇总，整理了报告导出，也把 Eat TI 的角色插画拆成了完整单图。

This release fixes collection and summaries on the English campus card site, updates report exports, and gives each Eat TI character a complete individual illustration.

## 英文采集 · English collection

- 英文页面的 `Transaction Successful!` 现在会被识别为成功交易，修复有流水却汇总为零的问题。

  `Transaction Successful!` is now recognized as a successful transaction, fixing reports that showed a zero total despite having records.

- 登录后的页面重定向或重新加载可触发有限次数的重试。查询表单、英文空列表和分页信息也更新了识别逻辑。

  Collection can retry a limited number of times after post-login redirects or reloads. Detection also covers the query form, English empty results, and pagination text.

- 取消或重新发起采集时，旧任务的结果不会覆盖新任务。英文界面会显示对应的错误说明。

  Results from an old collection job no longer overwrite a new job after cancellation or restart. The English interface also shows translated errors.

## 报告与导出 · Reports and exports

- 消费报告增加金额分布、餐段分布和 Fun Facts，支持中英切换与自定义日期。卡片保留月度走势，以图表和日常习惯为主，缩减辅助小字。

  Spending reports add amount distributions, meal-time breakdowns, and Fun Facts, with Chinese/English switching and custom dates. Cards retain monthly trends, focus on charts and everyday habits, and use less supporting text.

- Eat TI 的 25 个角色共用独立透明 PNG，网页、卡片和长图保留相同的插画细节。卡片只展示人格档案，长图保留统计、Fun Facts 和人格内容。

  All 25 Eat TI characters use individual transparent PNGs, preserving the same illustration details on the web, cards, and long images. Cards show character profiles, while long images retain statistics, Fun Facts, and profiles.

- 导出配色、字体和排版重新整理。各页面复用导出逻辑，卡片和长图按钮分别显示生成状态。

  Export colors, fonts, and layouts have been revised. Pages share the export logic, and the card and long-image buttons show their own generation state.

## 错峰角色保留上午 · Morning bites count as off-peak

「错峰干饭侠」合并统计 10:00–10:59、13:01–16:59 和 18:31 起的刷卡。合计次数在各餐段中最多时符合条件，并列也算。符合条件后仍参与最多 6 个人格的排序。

The Off Peak Diner combines swipes at 10:00–10:59, 13:01–16:59, and 18:31 onward. It qualifies when the combined count leads the meal groups, including ties, and then enters the ranking for up to six displayed characters.

## 下载与更新 · Download and update

下载适合电脑的 ZIP，并完整解压。Windows 双击 `启动.cmd`，Mac 双击 `启动.command`。包内自带 Node.js，需要已安装 Chrome 或 Edge。更新时先退出旧工具并备份 `private/`，将新版解压到新文件夹，再把旧版 `private/` 复制到新版目录后启动。

Download the ZIP for your computer and extract it completely. Open `启动.cmd` on Windows or `启动.command` on Mac. Node.js is included. Chrome or Edge must be installed. To update, exit the old tool and back up `private/`, extract the new ZIP into a separate folder, copy the old `private/` into it, then launch the new version.

Each ZIP has a matching `.sha256` checksum file. Mac packages require macOS 13.5+, are unsigned and unnotarized, and have not undergone native Mac launch or school sign-in verification.

The bilingual `使用说明.txt` in each Mac ZIP now explains first-launch approval, with an illustrated guide named `安全性设置方法.png`. After trying to open the file, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway for that file. The README includes the full steps.
